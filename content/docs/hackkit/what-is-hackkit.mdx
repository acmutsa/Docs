Hackkit is an all-in-one hackathon management platform that helps you plan, organize, and run hackathons with ease. It provides a range of features to make the hackathon experience smooth and enjoyable for participants and organizers alike, while remaining easy to setup and use.

## Features

- Schedule management
- Registration management
